crispy pizza recipe

ingredient

    Dough

  • Flour: 3 cups
  • Warm water: 1 cup
  • Yeast: 2¼ tsp
  • Sugar: 1 tsp
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Olive oil: 2 tbsp

    • Sauce

  • Tomato paste: ½ cup
  • Garlic: 2 cloves
  • Oregano: 1 tsp
  • Basil: 1 tsp
  • Salt + Sugar: ½ tsp each

    • Toppings - Dominion Special

  • Mozzarella cheese: 2 cups
  • Halal Chicken: 1 cup
  • Halal Beef: 1 cup
  • Bell peppers: ½ cup
  • Onions: ½ cup

    baking instrustions

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (232°C) and ensure it is fully preheated.
  2. Prepare the pizza crust by spreading the dough onto a pizza pan
    and baking just the crust for at least 5 minutes.
  3. Make the pizza sauce by mixing tomato purée and red wine vinegar, then adding minced garlic.
  4. Top the pizza with the sauce, cheese, and your preferred toppings.
  5. Bake the pizza for another 5–7 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the toppings are bubbly.
